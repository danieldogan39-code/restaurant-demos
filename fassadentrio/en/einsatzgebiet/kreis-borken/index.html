Facade cleaning in the Borken district and the Münsterland

FassadenTrio is based in Südlohn. From here we clean facades in all towns and municipalities of the Borken district and in the rest of the Münsterland.

Short distances in the western Münsterland

Our base is at Robert-Bosch-Straße 47 in Südlohn. From here we are with you quickly, for the initial consultation, for the free test patch and for the cleaning itself.

What we do for you on site

We remove algae, fungi, moss and dirt with low water pressure and then apply a transparent long-term protection that delays regrowth for up to 5 years. No scaffolding is needed for this. The three phases are shown on the Process page.

For owners, property managers and businesses

We clean single-family homes, apartment buildings, residential complexes, company buildings and care homes.

Beyond the district too

We work throughout the Münsterland, in North Rhine-Westphalia, nationwide and all over Europe. More on this on the Service area page.

At a glance

  • Up to 5 years of protection against regrowth

Frequently asked questions

Do you also come to Bocholt, Ahaus or Gronau?

Yes. We clean facades throughout the Borken district and beyond, all over Germany and Europe.

Are there travel costs?

No. We do not charge for travel.

Can I see the result beforehand?

Yes. We clean a test patch on your facade free of charge. That way you see the result on your own building before you decide.

What does the cleaning cost?

Facade cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting. We will tell you the price for your building in a free quote.

See the result on your own building

We clean a section of your facade free of charge. Then you decide whether we should clean the whole facade.
