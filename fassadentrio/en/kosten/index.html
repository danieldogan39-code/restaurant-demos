Why cleaning is cheaper than painting

For repainting, scaffolding is usually put up and the whole surface is recoated. With cleaning, neither is necessary: our technicians work with telescopic lances or from an aerial work platform, there are no scaffolding costs, and the work is completed in a short time. That is why cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting.

What the effort depends on

Every facade is different. With facade cleaning, these points usually determine the effort:

  • the size of the facade area,
  • the height of the building and how easily the facade can be reached,
  • the type and extent of the growth.

That is why we tell you the price for your building in a quote. It is free.

What costs nothing with us

  • the test patch on your facade,
  • the quote,
  • travel.

What the cleaning includes

The process has three phases: apply the cleaning agent, rinse, apply long-term protection. The protection delays regrowth for up to 5 years. The details are on the Process page.

Frequently asked questions

What does the cleaning cost?

Facade cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting. We will tell you the price for your building in a free quote.

What does the test patch cost?

Nothing. The test patch is free and you can request it at any time. We do not charge for the quote or for travel either.

Why not simply repaint?

Because cleaning is up to 70% cheaper and postpones repainting by many years. It is also more environmentally friendly than a complete repaint. In addition, painters do not usually give a guarantee against new algae growth when they repaint.

Are there costs for scaffolding?

No. We work with telescopic lances and, for tall buildings, with an aerial work platform. There are no scaffolding costs.

See the result on your own building

We clean a section of your facade free of charge. Then you decide whether we should clean the whole facade.
