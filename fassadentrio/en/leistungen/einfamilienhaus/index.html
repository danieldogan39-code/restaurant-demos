Facade cleaning for single-family homes

Green growth on the weather side, dark streaks below the window sills: we clean the facade of your home with low water pressure and then protect it against regrowth for up to 5 years.

Request a free test patchHow the cleaning works

When your own home turns grey and green

Algae and fungi settle first where the facade stays damp for a long time: on the weather side, below window sills and on surfaces in the shade of trees or neighbouring houses. The render underneath is often still in good condition. In that case your home does not need new paint but a thorough cleaning.

From the ground to the gable, without scaffolding in the garden

Our technicians work with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long. With these they usually reach the entire facade of a single-family home from the ground. You do not need scaffolding, and the cleaning is completed in a short time.

Test first, then decide

Before you commit, we clean a test patch on your facade free of charge. That way you see on your own home what the result looks like. The quote is also free, and we do not charge for travel.

What you gain

  • Cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting and postpones it by many years.
  • The transparent long-term protection delays regrowth for up to 5 years and can be renewed at any time.
  • Test patch, quote and travel are free.
  • Cleaning is more environmentally friendly than a complete repaint.

You can read how the cleaning works in detail on the Process page. You will find examples under References.

Frequently asked questions

Does scaffolding have to be put up?

No. Our technicians work from the ground with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long. For taller buildings we use an aerial work platform. There are no scaffolding costs.

Will the facade be damaged during cleaning?

No. We work with low water pressure. The cleaning agent loosens algae, mould and fungi, and we then rinse them off with clear water. The render stays undamaged.

What does it cost to clean a single-family home?

It costs up to 70% less than repainting. We will tell you the price for your home in a free quote.

How long does the facade stay clean?

After cleaning we apply a transparent long-term protection. It delays regrowth for up to 5 years. The protection can be reapplied at any time.
