Frequently asked questions about facade cleaning

Costs, procedure, durability: here you will find the answers at a glance. Your question is not listed? Give us a call.

Request a free test patch+49 178 812 08 73

Procedure and process

Will the facade be damaged during cleaning?

No. We work with low water pressure. The cleaning agent loosens algae, mould and fungi, and we then rinse them off with clear water. The render stays undamaged.

Does scaffolding have to be put up?

No. Our technicians work from the ground with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long. For taller buildings we use an aerial work platform. There are no scaffolding costs.

How do you clean facades that are taller than 12 metres?

Up to around 12 metres our technicians work from the ground with telescopic lances. We clean taller facades from an aerial work platform. No scaffolding is put up.

Can I clean the facade myself with a pressure washer?

We advise against it. High water pressure can damage the render. That is why our process uses low pressure, and the long-term protection delays regrowth.

How does the test patch work?

You request the test patch via the form or by phone. We clean a section of your facade. You see the result on your own building and then decide whether we should clean the whole facade.

Costs

What does the cleaning cost?

Facade cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting. We will tell you the price for your building in a free quote.

What does the test patch cost?

Nothing. The test patch is free and you can request it at any time. We do not charge for the quote or for travel either.

Why not simply repaint?

Because cleaning is up to 70% cheaper and postpones repainting by many years. It is also more environmentally friendly than a complete repaint. In addition, painters do not usually give a guarantee against new algae growth when they repaint.

Result and durability

How long does the facade stay clean?

After cleaning we apply a transparent long-term protection. It delays regrowth for up to 5 years. The protection can be reapplied at any time.

What do you remove from the facade?

Algae, mould, fungal growth, moss, lichen, fine dust, road dust, soot and grease.

Can the long-term protection be renewed?

Yes. The facade protection can be reapplied at any time.

Buildings and service area

Which buildings is the cleaning suitable for?

We clean single-family homes, apartment buildings, residential complexes, company buildings, care homes and prefab panel blocks.

Where does FassadenTrio work?

Our base is in Südlohn in the Münsterland region. We work throughout Germany and all over Europe.

Do you also clean the windows?

Yes, on request. We offer window cleaning following the facade cleaning.
