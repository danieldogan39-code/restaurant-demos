Three phases to a clean facade

The FassadenTrio process cleans quickly and postpones costly repainting by many years.

  1. Phase 1

    Apply the cleaning agent

    Our cleaning agent, developed specifically for facades, is applied over the whole surface: from the ground with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long, or from an aerial work platform. It loosens the growth; the facade is not yet clean at this stage.

  2. Phase 2

    Rinse the facade

    As soon as the algae, mould and fungi have loosened, we rinse them off with clear water. The pressure is so low that the render stays undamaged.

  3. Phase 3

    Apply long-term protection

    The transparent protection penetrates deep into the pores and delays regrowth for up to 5 years. It can be renewed at any time.

Why facades turn green

On the left you can see how to recognise growth. On the right you can read how it develops and why early cleaning protects the render.

  • Green growth

  • Dark streaks below windows

  • Grey film

Around 90% of all facade soiling is caused by microorganisms such as algae and fungi.

  • Condensation on the renderOn insulated facades the top coat of render is thermally decoupled from the masonry. It cools down, and condensation forms quickly.
  • Moisture as a breeding groundAlgae and fungal spores settle on the damp surface and spread.
  • Driving rain and short roof overhangsWhere rain hits the wall unhindered, the growth spreads particularly fast.
  • Consequences for the renderThe growth can attack the render, make it porous and leave deep discolouration. The earlier it is cleaned, the better.

Frequently asked questions

Will the cleaning damage my facade?

No. We work with low water pressure. The cleaning agent loosens algae, mould and fungi, and we then rinse them off with clear water. The render stays undamaged.

Does scaffolding have to be put up?

No. Our technicians work from the ground with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long. For taller buildings we use an aerial work platform. There are no scaffolding costs.

How long does the facade stay clean?

After cleaning we apply a transparent long-term protection. It delays regrowth for up to 5 years. The protection can be reapplied at any time.

What does the test patch cost?

Nothing. The test patch is free and you can request it at any time. We do not charge for the quote or for travel either.

What does the cleaning cost?

Facade cleaning costs up to 70% less than repainting. We will tell you the price for your building in a free quote.

How does the test patch work?

You request the test patch via the form or by phone. We clean a small section of your facade. You see the result on your own building and then decide whether we should clean the whole facade.

Why not simply repaint?

Because cleaning is up to 70% cheaper and postpones repainting by many years. It is also more environmentally friendly than a complete repaint. In addition, painters do not usually give a guarantee against new algae growth when they repaint.

Can I clean the facade myself with a pressure washer?

We advise against it. High water pressure can damage the render. That is why our process uses low pressure, and the long-term protection delays regrowth.

Which buildings is the cleaning suitable for?

We clean single-family homes, apartment buildings, residential complexes, company buildings, care homes and prefab panel blocks. On request we also clean the windows afterwards.

Where does FassadenTrio work?

Our base is in Südlohn in the Münsterland region. We work throughout Germany and all over Europe.
