Clean the facade with a pressure washer?

There is a pressure washer in many garages, and the green facade is an obvious target. We still advise against it. Here is why.

Request a free test patchTo the process

High pressure can damage the render

A pressure washer removes growth by force. Render is not made for that: the jet can roughen or damage the surface. A rough surface holds moisture for longer, and moisture is the breeding ground for algae and fungi.

The upper areas remain hard to reach

From the ground, a pressure washer does not reach the gable. If you want to go higher, you need a ladder or scaffolding. Our technicians work from the ground with telescopic lances up to 12 metres long.

Without protection the growth comes back

Spraying it off does not remove the cause. The facade gets damp again, and new spores settle. That is why our process includes a transparent long-term protection that delays regrowth for up to 5 years.

How it works with low pressure

In our process a specially developed cleaning agent loosens the growth. The water then only has to rinse it off. The pressure is so low that the render stays undamaged. The three phases are shown on the Process page.
