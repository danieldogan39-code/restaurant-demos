What the facade check can do

The check classifies what you see on your facade. Green growth, dark streaks and a grey film are mostly caused by algae and fungi. Around 90% of all facade soiling is due to such microorganisms. They sit on the surface, and that is where cleaning comes in.

What it cannot do

The check does not replace an inspection on site. It cannot see your facade; it only calculates with your details. The area is an estimate. We will tell you the price in a free quote.

The reliable way: the test patch

Only a test on your facade shows what it will look like after cleaning. The test patch is free. If you wish, we will carry your details from the check straight over into the request.
